🏝️How the game works

Six island teams trade to survive over about six rounds. Each round:

  1. Event: something happens (a storm, a drought, a bumper harvest, a pirate raid) that changes what goods are worth.
  2. Production: your island makes its goods. You will have lots of some things and none of others.
  3. Market: trade with other islands before the timer runs out. Agree your own prices.
  4. Settle up: pay your island's needs and a coin tax, then spend what is left building projects for points.

The team with the most points at the end wins. No island can win alone, so you will need to trade.

Teachers: a full lesson takes about 90 minutes for 18 to 30 students, with one device per team and a projector. Read the teacher guide for the lesson plan, student roles and debrief questions.
